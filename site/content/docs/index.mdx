Better Helpdesk is an open-source (MIT) support inbox, ticketing system and lightweight CRM that you install into your own Next.js app as an npm package, `better-helpdesk`. It is a library, not a service: nothing runs beside your app, and there is no second login, database or deploy to look after. Your customers write in through a widget on your site, your support team answers them from a page inside your app, and every conversation is stored in the database you already run: Postgres, MySQL, SQL Server or SQLite.

In these docs an **agent** is a member of your support team. It is never an AI. AI is an optional adapter, and what it produces is a suggestion or a draft that an agent reviews.

## The pieces

You add four things to your app, and that is the whole installation.

| Piece | What it is | Where it lives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The handler | One function from `Request` to `Response`, built by `buildHelpdesk()`, that serves every route of the helpdesk | A catch-all route such as `app/api/helpdesk/[...slug]/route.ts` |
| The agent UI | `HelpdeskAdmin`, a React component with the inbox, contacts, companies, deals and settings | A page of your app, such as `/helpdesk` |
| The widget | `<helpdesk-widget>`, a web component with a React wrapper and a standalone script | Any page of your site, or a site on another origin |
| The tables | The helpdesk's tables, created by `better-helpdesk-migrate` | Your own database |

Your app keeps the one thing a helpdesk usually takes from you: identity. A function you write, `identify(request)`, reads your own session and says who is asking and whether they are an agent. Better Helpdesk stores no passwords and no sessions.

Everything else is optional and arrives as an adapter you pass in: sending email, receiving it, storing attachments, AI suggestions and drafts, and searching your own help pages. Background work, such as reminders and outgoing mail, runs when your own scheduler calls it. Leave an adapter out and the feature it powers is not there.

## Who it is for

Better Helpdesk suits a team that already has a Next.js app with its own login and database, and wants support to live inside that product rather than next to it. It needs Next.js 15 or newer, Node.js 22.19 or newer, React 19, and PostgreSQL 14, MySQL 8.4 or SQL Server 2022 or newer, or SQLite. On another database, you can [write your own adapter](/docs/guides/own-adapter). Both interfaces ship in English and German. It is not built for support by phone or on social channels, or for a team that wants a hosted tool with its own logins.
